🏦 Monthly Bank Statement Import (Admin Only)

1. Download this month's statement from your bank's app/site. 2. Upload it below. 3. Click Analyze to preview, then Generate to add it to Collections & Expenses and save — everyone opening the shared file will see the update.
💡 Tip: ask residents to start the UPI remark/note with their flat number, e.g. "Flat 203 Maintenance" — put it first, since some bank apps truncate long notes. This lets the import auto-match the payment to the right flat.
