Capabilities

Everything a community needs

A complete operations toolkit — billing to reporting — in one dependable platform.

Maintenance billing

Auto-generate monthly bills per flat with configurable charges, due dates and one-click receipts.

Online payments

Residents pay by UPI and upload proof; admins verify and reconcile collections in real time.

Complaints tracking

Residents raise issues and follow status from open to resolved — nothing slips through the cracks.

Notices & announcements

Broadcast circulars and event notices to every resident instantly, with a permanent record.

Expenses & reports
